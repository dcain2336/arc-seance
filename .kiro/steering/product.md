# Steering — Product

ARC Séance is a Halloween terminal that makes dead technology fun again.
The audience is a curious visitor or a hackathon judge with five minutes.

Voice rules for every line the app prints:

- Friendly spooky, never gory, never scary at a real person.
- Short lines. The terminal is small, so say it in one breath.
- The séance-master narrates; spirits speak in their own voice.
- Never claim live data the app does not have. Say "sample" or "last
  fetched" when that is the truth.

Product rules:

- Free forever: no key, no account, no payment, no backend.
- Offline first: the web app works with its bundled data; the network is
  only for the optional fetcher and the optional IRC client.
- Honest labels: sample data says sample, stale data says its fetch date.
